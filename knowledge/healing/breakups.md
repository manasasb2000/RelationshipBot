# Make room for grief

Breakup grief can move unevenly. Reducing repeated exposure to messages and social feeds can create room for recovery without turning the past into an enemy. Maintain basic routines, use trusted support, and choose small commitments that reconnect you with identity outside the relationship.

# Contact decisions

Before contacting an ex, ask what outcome you expect, whether the other person has requested space, and how you will care for yourself if the answer disappoints you. A draft that is not sent can still help organize feelings. Respect explicit no-contact boundaries.
