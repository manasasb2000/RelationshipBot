# Couple and family systems

In many Indian families, decisions about marriage, living arrangements, caregiving, religion, finances, and celebrations involve more than the couple. Partners can respect family connection while still making their own agreements. Begin by deciding privately what is flexible, what needs joint consent, and what each partner will communicate to their own family.

# Arranged-marriage conversations

Early conversations can cover values, career plans, finances, children, caregiving expectations, household labor, location, religion, privacy, and conflict style. Compatibility is not proven by a checklist; notice whether both people can disagree respectfully and make room for each other’s agency.
