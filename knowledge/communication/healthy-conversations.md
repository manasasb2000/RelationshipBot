# Start softly

A difficult conversation is more likely to remain constructive when it begins with a specific observation, a feeling, and a request. Describe the event without assigning a motive: “When our plans changed and I did not hear from you, I felt unimportant. Could you send me a quick message next time?” Avoid global claims such as “you always” and “you never.”

# Repair during conflict

When either person becomes overwhelmed, agree on a pause and a clear time to return. A pause is not abandonment when it includes reassurance and a return plan. Useful repairs include acknowledging impact, correcting a harsh phrase, naming shared goals, and asking what would help the other person feel understood.
