# What a boundary is

A boundary states what a person will do to protect their wellbeing; it is not a threat designed to control another person. A clear boundary names the situation, the need, and the action: “I want to talk about this, and I will pause the conversation if we start insulting each other. We can try again when we are calm.”

# Following through

Consistency makes boundaries understandable. Start with a proportionate action that is under your control. When safety is a concern, prioritize distance, trusted support, and qualified local services over confrontation.
